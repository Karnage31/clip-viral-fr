Clip Viral FR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Clip Viral FR is a personal desktop tool used by a single creator to edit short video compilations on their own computer and to upload them to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok through Login Kit, Clip Viral FR receives, with their consent, only the data needed for the scopes they approve:

  • user.info.basic: display name, username and avatar, shown in the app so the owner can confirm which account they are posting to.
  • video.upload / video.publish: used only to upload videos that the owner has reviewed, approved and explicitly confirmed, as a draft or a post.
  • video.list: view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own videos, used to compare the performance of their own content.

How data is stored

All data stays on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager, not in files and not in the source code. Video statistics are stored in a local database file. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time from the app (this deletes the stored tokens) or revoke access in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions → Apps. Deleting the app's folder removes all local data.
