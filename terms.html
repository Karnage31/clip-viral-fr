Clip Viral FR – Terms of Service

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. What Clip Viral FR is

Clip Viral FR is a personal desktop tool that helps its owner edit vertical video compilations and upload them to the owner's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It is not a public service.

2. Content and rights

Only content that the owner has the right to reuse is processed: content provided by its creators with written permission, content under a license that allows reuse (such as Creative Commons BY, with attribution), or the owner's own content. Every video credits the original creators. Clip Viral FR does not circumvent any technical protection.

3. Posting

Nothing is posted without the owner's review and explicit confirmation of each video. The owner chooses the caption, the visibility and the interaction settings before each post, in line with TikTok's content sharing guidelines.

4. TikTok terms

Use of TikTok features through Clip Viral FR is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation.

5. No warranty

Clip Viral FR is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
